Data: csconferences.csv, from conference front matter, program chairs' reports, HotCRP, and other primary sources. Submission deadlines: ccf-deadlines (MIT; community-maintained), fetched live by your browser from ccfddl.com, with a saved snapshot as fallback, plus deadlines from official calls for papers; estimates (~) are projected from the previous deadline. Always confirm on the conference's official site. Counts exclude papers withdrawn or desk-rejected before review where sources distinguish them; long/full research papers only. Conference ranks: ICORE2026 (CORE with GII-GRIN-SCIE), CCF (2026, 7th edition), and Google Scholar Metrics h5-index. · GitHub · Corrections welcome via pull request.
